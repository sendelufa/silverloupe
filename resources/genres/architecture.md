# Architecture editorial guide

## Criteria

Compare geometry, line, space, light, material, composition, and the stated commercial or authorial purpose. Include establishing views, spatial relationships, and details when the brief requests them.

## Counterexamples and artistic exceptions

Converging verticals may be deliberate, and an abstract detail can be a required view. Protected HDR and panorama sources are judged as a future composite, not as disposable standalone frames.

## Escalation

Escalate uncertain perspective intent, incomplete protected sets, and conflicts between strict commercial geometry and an authorial series. Exceptions prevent automatic rejection but do not guarantee selection.

## Coverage

Coverage labels should come from the brief and observed evidence, such as exterior, interior, context, circulation, and material detail. Missing views remain explicit.

## Comparison with acceptable alternatives

A centered elevation and an oblique view can both be valid when they serve different coverage needs. Within a near-duplicate pair, retain alternatives when light and geometry trade off without a clear winner.
