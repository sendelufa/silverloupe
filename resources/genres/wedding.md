# Wedding editorial guide

## Criteria

Prefer meaningful moments, expression, interaction, composition, light, and a coherent event sequence. A technically cleaner frame is not automatically stronger than the only photograph of a significant moment.

## Counterexamples and artistic exceptions

Closed eyes during a kiss or embrace and motion during dancing can support the action. Do not infer identities, roles, or relationships from faces, and do not invent events absent from the brief.

## Escalation

Escalate unique moments, uncertain eye context, accidental occlusion, and unresolved ties for review. An exception removes an unconditional penalty; it does not prove that the frame succeeds.

## Coverage

Use only the user's checklist. When requested coverage cannot fit K or is absent from the observed shoot, report the conflict rather than fabricating a scene.

## Comparison with acceptable alternatives

Two frames of the same exchange may both be acceptable when one has the stronger expression and another better shows the interaction. Keep both as alternatives when the evidence does not establish a single winner.
